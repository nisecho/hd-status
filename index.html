How this page works

A watcher outside our systems checks each service about every 5 minutes. A check can be a few minutes late. Uptime is the share of checks that found the service working. An incident is two or more failed checks in a row. This page is not a part of the website, so it also loads when the website does not.

All times are US Central time. The website is https://app.homedoctors4u.com, the agency portal is https://hhn.homedoctors4u.com, and our public website is https://www.homedoctors4u.com.
